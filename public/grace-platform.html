04 · Accountable by design

The work stays clear. People stay accountable.

GRACE is built to make the work of ministry easier to operate and easier to explain: access is scoped, important activity leaves a record, and operating assumptions remain visible.

Access

Records are scoped to the role.

People work from the records their responsibility requires, rather than a single, unrestricted church database.

Decisions

Automation does not replace judgment.

Care and operational workflows are designed around clear roles and human responsibility, so a recommendation never becomes an unowned decision.

Stewardship

The assumptions stay inspectable.

GRACE keeps operating estimates separate from future Impact Card potential until the required program agreements exist.
