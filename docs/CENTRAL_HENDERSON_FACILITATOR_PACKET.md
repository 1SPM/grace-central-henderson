## Objectives (say them to yourself before walking in)

Leave with: agreed pilot outcomes · 3–5 selected workflows · named systems
of record + owners · sensitive-data boundaries · permission requirements ·
an itemized owed-sources list · a populated Decision Log · a scheduled
next meeting. Anything less gets an owner and a date, never an invention.

## The six workflow candidates (Phase E shortlist)

1. Consent-aware send (communications)
2. Honest giving answers (giving/finance)
3. Current care picture — fresh, not stale (pastoral care)
4. Operational pulse from an authorized Henderson source (leadership)
5. Real group pulse (ministry/groups)
6. **Staff workday (anchor — already works end-to-end; the adoption on-ramp)**

Select 3–5 in the room against the six criteria (useful / frequent / data
/ permission / demo-able / feasible). A candidate failing data or
permission gets logged as deferred with an owner — no debate.

## Rules that must not bend in the room

- **Evidence tiers on every capture**: Told / Demonstrated / Source named /
  Approved. Someone *saying* something never makes it GRACE knowledge.
- **Decisions get logged** (ID, owner, date) or they didn't happen.
- **Feature ideas go to the parking lot** — by name, immediately, without
  debating merit. "Great — parking lot, your name on it."
- **Sensitive specifics stay out of the workbook**: capture who's
  *allowed* to see care/giving detail, never the detail itself.
- **Claim only what the Evidence Package says is proven.** The demo
  working once upgrades nothing.
- **A failed demo step is stated plainly and recovered per the runbook** —
  never faked, never compensated for with a riskier action.
- **No internal language**: no fixture IDs, test names, migrations,
  permission internals, or security-finding details in front of Central.
- **Close every section by reading its exit decision aloud** and getting a
  verbal yes; close the day by reading the exit checklist.

## If the day drifts

- Brainstorming spiral → parking lot, then restate the current phase's
  exit decision.
- "GRACE should know everything" → point back to the boundary demo:
  *"Everything she knows entered through a door you approved. Today we
  choose the doors."*
- A section running long → cut breadth, not the exit decision; unfinished
  categories get owners and follow-ups on the Control Sheet.
- Leadership pulled away → pause Phase D/F decisions rather than deciding
  without authority; reschedule that slice before everyone disperses.
