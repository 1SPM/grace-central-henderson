# GRACE Discovery Workshop — Who We'd Love in the Room

*For Central Henderson leadership — to help you decide who should join
which parts of the day. Roles, not names: you know your team.*

Nobody needs to attend all day. The schedule (see the agenda) is built so
each person joins the sessions where their knowledge or decision matters.

## Needed in the room

| Role | Sessions | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Senior/executive pastoral leadership** | Opening & outcomes (9:15) · Permissions & boundaries (12:45) · Decisions (14:40) | The day's real decisions — what the pilot should accomplish, who may see sensitive information, what's in and out — are leadership calls. |
| **Operations lead** | Most of the day | The connective tissue: how work actually flows at Central. |
| **Finance / giving records owner** | Where information lives (11:15) · Permissions & boundaries (12:45) | Giving is the most sensitive area we'll discuss — we need the person who actually owns those records. |
| **Pastoral care / prayer workflow owner** | Show-us session (10:00) · Permissions (12:45) · Where GRACE helps (13:30) | Care and prayer workflows are central to the pilot conversation. |
| **Database / records administrator** | Show-us (10:00) · Where information lives (11:15) · Where GRACE helps (13:30) | The person who actually enters and maintains records day-to-day — often knows things nobody else does. |
| **Communications owner** | Show-us (10:00) · Where information lives (11:15) · Permissions (12:45) · Where GRACE helps (13:30) | How messages get approved, sent, and who's opted out. |

## What to have available (not prepared — just accessible)

- Sign-in access to the systems your team uses daily (membership records,
  giving platform, scheduling, messaging) — several sessions ask you to
  *show* a real workflow on screen rather than describe it.
- Nothing else. No documents to write, no data to export.

## One decision we'd like your guidance on

The conversation about **who may see and ask about giving and pastoral
care information** is the most sensitive part of the day. Two good ways to
handle it — your call:

**Option A — a private leadership conversation first.** A short (30–45
min) session with senior leadership before the workshop, so those
boundaries are settled quietly and the workshop simply records them.
*Upside: candor, no staff present for a sensitive discussion. Cost: one
extra meeting.*

**Option B — inside the workshop (12:45 session).** Discussed with
leadership, finance, and care leads together in the room.
*Upside: everyone hears the same decision at once, one meeting. Cost: some
leaders prefer not to discuss giving-visibility norms in a wider group.*

Neither is wrong — tell us which fits Central's culture and we'll shape
the agenda accordingly.
