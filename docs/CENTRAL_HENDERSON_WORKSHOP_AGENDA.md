# GRACE Discovery Workshop — Central Henderson

*A working session with the VWS team — one day, with lunch provided by us.*

**What this day is:** a working conversation about how Central Henderson
actually operates, so that GRACE — the assistant we're piloting with your
staff — works from *your* truth: your records, your systems, your rules
about who sees what. You'll see GRACE live, and by the end of the day
we'll have decided together exactly what it will and won't do in the pilot.

**What this day is not:** a sales pitch, a tech deep-dive, or a commitment
to anything beyond the pilot we scope together.

**What to have handy:** access to the systems your team uses day-to-day
(membership records, giving, scheduling, messaging) — several sessions ask
you to *show* us a real workflow rather than describe it. No preparation
documents needed.

---

## Schedule

**9:00 — Welcome & a short introduction to GRACE** *(15 min)*
A live, five-minute look at GRACE answering real questions — including one
it correctly declines to answer, which we'll explain.
*Everyone.*

**9:15 — What should this pilot accomplish?** *(45 min)*
What would make this pilot a clear win for Central? What would your staff
actually use? What does success look like in your words?
*Leadership, operations — everyone welcome.*

**10:00 — Show us how Central works** *(60 min)*
Not a questionnaire — you drive. Show us, on screen: how a new person
enters your records, how prayer requests are handled, how volunteers get
scheduled, how a church-wide message goes out, and a few more.
*The people who do these tasks day-to-day.*

**11:00 — Break** *(15 min)*

**11:15 — Where does the real information live?** *(45 min)*
For each area of church life: which system is the true record, who owns
it, and how it stays current.
*Operations, records/database admin, finance, ministry leads.*

**12:00 — Lunch** *(45 min)*

**12:45 — Who's allowed to see, change, and approve?** *(45 min)*
The most important conversation of the day: for sensitive areas — giving,
care, prayer, family information — who may see it, who may ask GRACE about
it, and who signs off before anything changes. "We haven't decided" is a
perfectly good answer; we'll write down whose decision it is.
*Leadership, finance, pastoral care.*

**13:30 — Where GRACE helps first** *(60 min)*
We bring a shortlist of candidate ways GRACE could help your staff in the
pilot. Together we pick the three to five that matter most — judged by
your priorities, not ours.
*Leadership, operations, and the staff who'd actually use each one.*

**14:30 — Break** *(10 min)*

**14:40 — Decisions & next steps** *(45 min)*
We read back everything decided today, list what each side owes the other
(with names and dates), and set the follow-up meeting.
*Leadership, operations.*

**15:25 — Close** *(5 min)*
