# Before We Meet — A Short Pre-Read for Central Henderson

*Five minutes of reading, no preparation required.*

## What GRACE is

GRACE is an assistant for your staff. It answers questions from your
church's real records, remembers what your team tells it, and — when asked
to change something — proposes the change for a person to approve rather
than acting on its own.

Three things make it different from a generic AI chatbot:

1. **It only knows what you've authorized.** GRACE doesn't answer from the
   internet or from guesswork. Everything it knows about Central Henderson
   entered through a source your leadership approved.
2. **It knows what it doesn't know.** Ask it something it has no
   authorized source for, and it says so — plainly — instead of producing
   a confident-sounding guess. You'll see this live; it's our favorite
   part of the demo.
3. **It asks permission.** Anything consequential routes to a person for
   approval. Who those people are, for which actions, is one of the things
   we'll decide together.

## What the workshop is for

Before GRACE can be genuinely useful to your staff, it needs to understand
how *Central Henderson actually works* — not how church software assumes
churches work. In one working day, we'll:

- agree on what a successful pilot looks like, in your words;
- watch how your team really does things (you'll show us, on screen);
- map where your true records live and who owns them;
- decide who may see, ask about, and approve the sensitive things —
  giving, pastoral care, prayer, family information;
- pick the three to five ways GRACE should help your staff first.

## Why we'll ask so much about systems and ownership

GRACE is only as truthful as its sources. When it answers a question about
attendance or giving or a prayer request, that answer has to trace to a
record your team trusts — and to rules about who's allowed to see it.
Mapping that is most of the day. It can feel like a lot of "where does
this live, who owns it" — that's deliberate, and it's the reason GRACE
won't guess.

## What participants should expect to do

- Talk about how your area really works, including the messy parts —
  "honestly, that lives in a spreadsheet nobody updates" is a genuinely
  helpful answer.
- Show a few real workflows on screen (adding a person, sending a
  message, handling a prayer request).
- Say "we haven't decided that" freely — we'll write down whose decision
  it is rather than pressing for one on the spot.

## What this session is not

- Not a sales pitch — the pilot's scope shrinks as often as it grows in
  these conversations, and that's healthy.
- Not a technical deep-dive — no jargon, no architecture diagrams.
- Not a commitment beyond the pilot we scope together.
- Not a data-collection exercise — we won't take copies of your records;
  we're mapping where they live and who governs them. Anything GRACE later
  uses goes through your explicit approval first.
